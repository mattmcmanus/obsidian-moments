## Notes

### [[2026-02-12]] February meeting - The Left Hand of Darkness

Great discussion. Priya brought up the connection between the shifgrethor concept and modern ideas about gender performance. Mike thought the pacing was slow in the middle chapters but came around by the end.

Next month: **The Dispossessed**

### [[2026-02-05]] January meeting - Piranesi

Everyone loved it. Short but dense. We spent most of the time discussing the nature of the House and whether it's meant to be literal or metaphorical.

Jordan's theory about the tidal rooms mapping to stages of grief was fascinating.
